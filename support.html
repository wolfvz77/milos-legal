MILOS — Support

Welcome to MILOS support. We're here to help you get the most out of your recovery, sleep, and training.

Getting Started

Connect your data. On first launch, allow MILOS to read from Apple Health so it can calculate your recovery, sleep, strain, and stress. If you have an Apple Watch, pair it for live heart rate and breathwork haptics. No wearable yet? MILOS still works with the data available and shows sample insights so you can explore every feature.

Read your dashboard. The Home screen shows your Recovery score plus HRV, resting heart rate, strain, stress, and sleep. Tap any card to see what the score means, how it's calculated, and what it affects.

Use your coach. The morning Coach Brief summarizes where you stand and what to do. Tap "Ask coach" to ask follow-up questions anytime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does MILOS need HealthKit access?

MILOS reads metrics like heart rate variability, resting heart rate, and sleep to calculate your scores and personalize coaching. You can review or change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → MILOS.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. The iPhone app works on its own. An Apple Watch adds live heart-rate monitoring and haptic guidance during breathwork sessions.

How is my health data protected?

Your health data is encrypted on your device. If iCloud sync is on, it stays in your private iCloud account. We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

What is the Episode Check-In / breathwork feature?

When your wearable shows signs of a stress response, MILOS can offer a guided breathing session (Physiological Sigh, 4-7-8, or Box Breathing) with on-wrist haptics to help you settle. You can also start breathwork anytime from the app.

How do I export my data or delete my account?

Open the Account panel (avatar, top-right of Home) → Settings to export your data or delete your account.

MILOS isn't a medical device, right?

Correct. MILOS is a wellness and coaching tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.
