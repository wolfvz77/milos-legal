MILOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 06, 2026

MILOS ("MILOS," "we," "us," or "our") is a wellness and fitness coaching app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We built MILOS to be private by design: your health data stays encrypted on your device, and we never sell it.

By using MILOS, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name and email address so we can identify your account and sync your data. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the information you choose to share with us through Apple.

Health and fitness data. With your explicit permission, MILOS reads data from Apple Health (HealthKit), which may include heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleep analysis, workouts, steps, and active energy. MILOS uses this data to calculate your recovery, sleep, strain, and stress scores and to generate coaching guidance. With your permission, MILOS may also write workout data back to Apple Health.

Information you enter. This includes your profile (age, biological sex, height, weight, goals, training preferences, health conditions), journal entries, logged habits and triggers, nutrition entries, and progress photos.

Location. If you enable weather-aware training, MILOS uses your device location to retrieve local weather conditions. Location is used only for this feature and is not stored on our servers.

Device and usage information. We may collect basic diagnostic and usage information to keep the app running reliably.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Provide the app's core functionality — recovery, sleep, strain, and stress analysis; coaching; adaptive training; nutrition guidance; journaling; and breathwork.
  • Personalize your experience and recommendations.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the app.

We do not use your information for third-party advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. How Your Information Is Stored and Protected

  • On-device encryption. Sensitive health data is encrypted on your device and protected by iOS Data Protection.
  • iCloud sync. If enabled, your data syncs through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We do not have access to the contents of your iCloud database.
  • HealthKit data is never used for advertising or marketing, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties for their own purposes, consistent with Apple's HealthKit requirements.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your health data with advertisers or data brokers. We may share information only:

  • With service providers who help us operate the app, under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • If required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public.
  • In connection with a business transfer (e.g., merger or acquisition), with notice to you.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Permissions. You control HealthKit, location, camera, and photo access in iOS Settings and can revoke them at any time.
  • Access and deletion. You can export your data from within the app, and you can delete your account and associated data. Deleting the app removes locally stored data from your device.
  • Regional rights. Depending on where you live (e.g., the EU/EEA under GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

MILOS is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Medical Disclaimer

MILOS is a wellness and coaching tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition. The scores, insights, and recommendations are for general wellness and informational purposes only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated in-app where appropriate.
